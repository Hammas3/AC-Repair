Common AC Problems

Identify typical air conditioner issues that require technical attention and proper servicing.

AC Not Cooling

Blowing ambient or warm air instead of chilling.

Weak Airflow

Low air output from indoor unit vents.

Water Leakage

Water dripping from the indoor wall unit.

Unusual Noise

Bumping, rattling, or squealing sounds.

AC Not Turning On

Unit fails to respond or power up.

Bad Odor

Musty or unpleasant smell while running.

Ice/Frost Buildup

Ice forming on coils or refrigerant pipes.

Frequent Shutdown

Unit abruptly turns off repeatedly.

Remote/Control Issues

Failure to respond to remote commands.

Uneven Cooling

Inconsistent temperatures across rooms.

Helpful Advice

AC Maintenance Tips

Simple best practices to maintain your air conditioner's efficiency and longevity.

Keep filters clean

Regularly wash or clean your indoor air filters to prevent dust buildup and maintain airflow.

Keep airflow unobstructed

Ensure furniture, curtains, or objects do not block indoor vents or outdoor condenser units.

Pay attention to unusual sounds

If you hear clicking, squealing, or grinding noises, have a technician inspect the unit promptly.

Address water leakage early

Dripping water from indoor units indicates clogged drain pipes that require clearing.

Schedule maintenance when needed

Periodic tune-ups before summer seasons help ensure uninterrupted cooling performance.

Avoid ignoring cooling issues

Promptly addressing minor temperature drops prevents bigger compressor or gas issues later.
